How it works
Final needed = (Desired − Current × Current Weight%) ÷ Final Exam Weight%

Example: If you have 88% so far covering 70% of your grade, and your final is worth 30%, and you want 90%:
(90 − 88 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = 95.3% needed on the final.
